My league

Reads your Yahoo league's rosters with your own Yahoo login and saves them on this device only. Nothing is sent to a server or stored anywhere else, and the site shows league features only on devices that have synced.

On this device

Computer (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)

  1. Show the bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or ⌘+Shift+B on a Mac).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar:
    Sync to Sixth Man
  3. To sync: open your league on Yahoo (signed in), click the bookmark. Sixth Man opens with your league loaded.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap · bookmark v6
  2. Bookmark any page (Share → Add Bookmark), name it Sync to Sixth Man, save.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark, replace its address with the copied code, Done.
  4. To sync: open your league at basketball.fantasysports.yahoo.com in Safari (signed in), tap the address bar, type Sync and pick the bookmark (or open it from Bookmarks).

EFB projections (your subscription)

Reads Elite Fantasy Basketball's Category League Projections with your own login and saves them on this device only, for the hidden Players › EFB page. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere else.

Uses the same Sync to Sixth Man bookmark as your league (v6 and later): open elitefantasybasketball.com/category-league-projections (signed in), wait for the table, then run it. On EFB it saves the projections; on Yahoo it syncs your league. Sixth Man opens on Players › EFB.

Notes

Each device and browser keeps its own copy: sync on each one you use, on the same address (sixthmanhoops.app). Re-sync after waivers or trades. If Yahoo changes its pages the sync may stop working until it's updated.
