Value is a player's average z-score across your active categories, relative to everyone in the comparison pool who played that day. FG% and FT% count makes above or below average on their attempts, so volume matters. Turnovers count against. Tap a player to see the z-score under each stat.

Szn / 14 / 7 / 3G beside each name are 9-cat ranks: per-game over the season and the last 14 and 7 days, and over the team's last 3 games with any game the player missed counted as zero, so resting doesn't flatter anyone. All are against a top-130 pool with your punts applied, rebuilt every morning through last night. Top 100 is green.

Movers. A green 3G pill means the last-3 rank is at least 40% better than the season rank (inside the top 130); red means at least 40% worse for someone inside 130 on the season. ▲n after minutes means n more minutes a game over the last 3 than before them, usually the first sign of a new role. The Movers sort shows only flagged players.

Buy/Sell asks whether a start is real by comparing this season's per-minute numbers with last season's. Tap the i on that tab for the full explanation.

Buzz is Yahoo's most added and dropped players for the day across all Yahoo leagues, next to your Szn / 3G / Exp ranks. Follow or Fade comes only from those numbers and the minutes trend: a real minutes jump or an Exp inside 130 says follow; a season and Exp outside 130 with no new minutes says fade. For drops, Panic means the numbers say he's still worth a spot and Agree means the role really shrank. It knows nothing about news, so a Check (missed games) is worth a look.
